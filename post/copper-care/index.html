How to Care for and Clean Oxidized Copper Jewelry

 Posted on August 14, 2026  | 

Oxidized copper jewelry has a beautiful, antique elven charm, but it requires proper care to keep its mystical look. Over time, raw copper reacts with air and skin oils, which is a completely natural process.

3 Simple Rules to Protect Your Copper Talismans:

  1. Keep it Dry: Always remove your copper rings and earrings before swimming, showering, or washing your hands.
  2. Avoid Chemicals: Perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays can damage the beautiful protective patina. Apply your cosmetics before putting on your jewelry.
  3. Store Smart: When you are not wearing your jewelry, keep it in an airtight plastic bag or a dry jewelry box.

How to Clean Your Jewelry safely:

If your talisman starts to lose its shine, gently wipe it with a soft polishing cloth. Do not use harsh chemical cleaners, as they will completely strip away the dark, beautiful antiqued details.
